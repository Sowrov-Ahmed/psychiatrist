    §05How care works

    Four steps, no hurry

    01

    Talk

    You describe what has been happening in your own words. The first visit is a conversation, not a questionnaire, and nothing is rushed.

    02

    Assess

    He looks at sleep, health, family and work together. Tests are ordered only when they will change the plan, and you leave knowing what is going on.

    03

    Plan

    Options may include talking therapy, medicine or both. He explains the benefits, side effects and costs, and the final choice is yours.

    04

    Follow up

    Follow-up visits track progress, adjust treatment and plan for difficult weeks. Family can join you whenever you want them to.
