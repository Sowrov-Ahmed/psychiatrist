§02

Answers here are for learning, not for emergencies

This page

It explains how care works and what to expect. It cannot diagnose you, and it does not replace a conversation with a doctor who knows your history.

In a crisis

If you or someone near you may be in danger, call 999 or go to the nearest hospital emergency department now. Do not wait for an appointment.

Not urgent, but unsure

Call the appointment desk, Saturday to Thursday. A person will help you choose the right chamber, or the right time to book an online visit.
