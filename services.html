§02

Three steps, in order

Every service follows the same pathway. Nothing is prescribed before it is explained, and nothing continues without a review.

01

Talk first

A long first visit. You describe what is happening, Dr. Rashed asks questions and explains his thinking in plain language. You leave knowing what he suspects and what he does not yet know.

Step one · Assess

02

Treat proportionately

The lightest treatment that can work comes first: therapy, sleep and routine changes, family guidance. Medicine is added when it will help, with side effects and timelines explained.

Step two · Agree a plan

03

Review regularly

Visits are scheduled to check what is working. Plans are adjusted, shortened or ended when you are ready. A written plan covers what to do if symptoms return.

Step three · Follow up
