§07Before your first visit

What to bring

You do not need to prepare much. A few papers and a few notes help the doctor understand you quickly, so the visit is spent talking, not searching for records. Tick items as you pack them.

§08Our promise

What you say stays here

01

A private file

Your records are kept in a locked cabinet or a password-protected system. Only the doctor and the desk staff who need them can open your file.

02

No recordings

Sessions are never recorded, in the chamber or online. The waiting areas have no cameras pointed at the consultation corridor.

03

You decide who knows

Family, employers and schools hear nothing without your consent. For children and teenagers, the doctor explains clearly what parents will be told.

04

One honest exception

If there is a serious risk to someone’s safety, the doctor may need to involve others. He tells you this at the start, so you always know where you stand.

If you or someone near you is in immediate danger, call 999 or go to the nearest hospital emergency department. Please do not wait for an appointment.
